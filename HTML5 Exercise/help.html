Help & Support

Welcome to the help center for the Local Community Event Portal.

  • Registering for an event: Navigate to the Home page and scroll down to the "Event Registration Form". Fill out all required fields and click submit.
  • Viewing past events: Double-click on any image in the gallery to enlarge it.
  • Finding nearby events: Click the "Find Nearby Events" button and allow location permissions in your browser.
